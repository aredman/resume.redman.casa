Activities

USS Little Rock

The Radio Association of Western NY (RAWNY) clubhouse is hosted aboard the USS Little Rock in the Buffalo Naval Park Museum. W2PE club members operate from there most weekends and open the radio room to the public acting as ambassadors amateur radio and sharing part of the ships history with the public.

Parks on the Air (POTA)

I've done a bit of Parks on the Air, mostly on the Erie Canal Hertiage Corridor park #US-6532 and often at the W2PE clubhouse aboard the USS Little Rock.
